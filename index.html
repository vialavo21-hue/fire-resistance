Raid Resistance Damage Matrix

Raid resistance scales the damage you deal to each boss and the damage your corps take. Each boss and difficulty has its own floor and ceiling, and resistance moves you in a straight line between them. Past the ceiling nothing changes. The axis is logarithmic, because full value runs from 160 resistance on difficulty 1 up to 13,600 on difficulty 4. Hover or tap any heatmap for the exact numbers at that resistance.

Each raid: 5 bosses × 4 difficulties · 10 to 20,000 resistance at 10-point resolution, log axis. Below each row's lower threshold the multipliers stay at their worst value, so 0 resistance reads the same as the left edge. Dotted lines mark what the raid's priestess pet gives as raid pet with 0 pieces: grade 1 300, grade 2 1,100, grade 3 2,400, grade 4 5,000, grade 5 9,700.
